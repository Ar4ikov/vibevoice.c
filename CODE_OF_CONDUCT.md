# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people. Assume the person on the other
side is trying to make the thing work.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism technical and specific. "This reads past the buffer when the
  header is 4 KB" is useful; "this is garbage" is not.
- Take correction the same way: a review that finds a bug did you a favour.
- Say what you measured and on what. Confidence without numbers wastes
  everyone's time.
- English in issues, pull requests and code comments, so that the discussion
  stays readable to whoever shows up next.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, insults about who someone is rather than what
they wrote, sexualised language or imagery, publishing anyone's private
information, and sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and commits in this repository, and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report it to the maintainer: [bizy18588@gmail.com](mailto:bizy18588@gmail.com),
or privately through
[GitHub's advisory form](https://github.com/Ar4ikov/vibevoice.c/security/advisories/new)
if it involves something that should not be public. Reports are read by the
maintainer and answered; what happens next depends on the case and can be
anything from a word in a thread to a block.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short on purpose.
